[Normal] (Align: None) While working on the project, I encountered both conceptual and practical challenges that pushed me to think deeper and debug smarter:

[Normal] (Align: LEFT (0)) Mark Matching Logic (Subset Problem): Selecting a valid subset of questions that exactly matched the total marks became a mini-algorithm challenge. I implemented a brute-force approach and optimized it with early exits to avoid long execution time for large input sets.
